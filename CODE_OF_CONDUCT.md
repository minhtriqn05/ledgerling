# Code of Conduct

Ledgerling follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome questions from beginners, criticise ideas and code, never people, and accept
that the maintainer may say no. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not accepted in issues, pull
requests or any other place of this project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer privately through the contact on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/minhtriqn05), or open
a private report in the **Security** tab when the problem is about safety. Every report is read and kept confidential.
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block accounts that break this code.
